Budget and spending

Source

Data: County financial system budget export, exported 2026-11-18. Static, read from the export, not live. Pages built November 28, 2026.

  • County financial system budget export, BG Account Inquiry All(BG Account Inquiry All)(1).csv, exported 2026-11-18
  • Names and structure: the County chart of accounts (COA Object List and COA Org with Long Account Mapping), with shorthand written out where the County has approved it
  • Service areas: the County list of departments in each service area

Notes

  • Revenue amounts are positive. Expense amounts are as the export gives them, so a credit can be negative.
  • Spending leaves out transfers to other funds and pass-through payments; Revenue leaves out transfers from other funds and pass-through receipts. Each is its own measure.
  • Net County cost is computed here: expenses less revenues in the General Fund and HHS Operations (funds 1000 and 2800).
  • Spent so far and Received so far for FY 2026-27 are to the date of the export.

Notes

  • Figures are whole dollars as the book prints them.
  • Projects are listed as the book lists them; the book names no department or fund for a project.
  • On a project, funding in the plan is its prior approvals plus the five programmed years; programmed is the five years only.
  • A project's cost estimate is its funding in the plan plus the additional funds still needed.

The projects listed under Civic Center do not add up to the totals the book prints for the same years. The projects are shown as the book lists them.

Where the book prints a total, that total is shown (the Capital improvement plan page, and Stats while nothing is filtered); a filtered selection adds up the projects listed. Over the five years the projects add up to $541,800,128 and the book prints $542,300,128. By year the projects differ from the book by +$1,750,000 in FY27, −$2,250,000 in FY28.
